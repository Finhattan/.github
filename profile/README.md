**Forward deployed engineering firm building on-chain infrastructure inside banks and financial institutions.**

Finhattan deploys, adapts and operates, inside each bank, the technology its retail clients need to access digital assets: trading and investing in digital assets and tokenised stocks, custody and transfers via regulated stablecoins, all from the institution's own app and under its brand.

### What we cover
- Custody and key management in the bank's own HSMs, physical or in its cloud
- Execution and connectivity with blockchain networks
- Integration with core banking and the accounting ledger
- A modular client experience inside the bank's app: multi-asset portfolio charts with every buy and sell point, AI-powered market analysis, a risk-free investment simulator, a social layer, chats, news, video and podcasts

The infrastructure runs within the institution's perimeter and under its control. The bank keeps the client, the brand, the licences and the control.
